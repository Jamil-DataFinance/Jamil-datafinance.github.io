01The problem

Two processes, one set of numbers, and nobody can trace between them

In most groups each company's results come from its own trial balance, while the consolidation lives in a separate spreadsheet: intercompany eliminations, investment eliminations and minority-interest calculations keyed by hand. That means two processes to maintain, extra reconciliation every month, and group numbers that are hard to trace back to the companies underneath.

02My approach

One model that answers both questions

  1. One chart of accounts for every entityAll trial balances mapped to a common structure, so entity and group statements come from the same data.
  2. Select one entity: stand-alone resultsIts own P&L and balance sheet, including intercompany sales and balances.
  3. Select all: the consolidated groupIntercompany activity and the parent's investment are eliminated automatically against subsidiary equity.
  4. Calculate NCI inside the modelProfit and equity attributable to the 30% minority in Alpha Trading, not in a side spreadsheet.
  5. Show every adjustmentP&L and balance sheet bridges list each entity, each elimination and the group total, so every number is auditable.
