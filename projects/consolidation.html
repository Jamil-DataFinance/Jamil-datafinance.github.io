Individual & Consolidated Financial Statements in Power BI

Built a single Power BI solution to view individual entity financial statements and consolidated group financial statements in one place, with intercompany eliminations and non-controlling interest adjustments.

The problem

Entity reporting and group consolidation usually live apart

In most groups, each company's results come from its own trial balance, while the consolidation is done separately in spreadsheets: intercompany eliminations, investment eliminations and NCI calculations kept by hand.

That means two processes to maintain, extra reconciliation work, and consolidated numbers that are hard to trace back to the companies underneath.

What I built

A single model that answers both questions

  • Select one entity to see its standalone results, including its intercompany sales and balances.
  • Select "All" to see the consolidated group: intercompany activity is removed automatically.
  • The parent's investment in subsidiaries is eliminated against the subsidiaries' equity.
  • Non-controlling interest is calculated for the 70%-owned subsidiary, not kept in a side spreadsheet.
  • Consolidation bridges show every adjustment, so the group numbers can be audited.

Why it matters

What this shows

  • Practical knowledge of IFRS 10 consolidation: control, eliminations, and NCI.
  • Reporting design that removes manual spreadsheet steps from the month-end consolidation.
  • Transparency: consolidated figures always reconcile back to each entity's books.
  • A structure that scales: new entities can be added without rebuilding the report.
