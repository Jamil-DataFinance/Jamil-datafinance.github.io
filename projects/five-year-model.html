Integrated 5-Year Financial Modelling & Valuation

An integrated five-year financial model in Excel linking the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement, with scenario analysis, sensitivity tables and supporting DCF valuation schedules to assess business performance and enterprise value under different assumptions.

The purpose

A planning tool for management and investors

A five-year projection answers the questions boards and lenders ask: how fast can the business grow, what will it earn, and will it generate enough cash to fund itself and repay its debt?

How it's built
  • Historical actuals set the baseline for every forecast assumption.
  • The three statements are fully linked: profit flows into retained earnings, and cash flow drives the closing cash balance.
  • Supporting schedules for depreciation, debt and working capital drive the forecast.
  • Inputs and assumptions such as inflation are separated from calculations, so scenarios are easy to run.
  • A one-page dashboard summarises the results for an executive reader.
