FP&A & Financial Performance Dashboard

A complete monthly performance pack for a fictional e-commerce business, from revenue to EBITDA. It ends with a what-if model where management can change assumptions and see the EBITDA impact instantly.

BusinessE-commerce company, FY2025
Pages6 report pages
CoversBudget vs Actual, margins, AR aging, scenarios
Built withPower BI, DAX (40+ measures)
The problem

Management needs more than sales charts

A finance director wants to know: are we on budget, which products and channels actually make money, which customers are slow to pay, and what happens to EBITDA if growth slows or costs rise?

Those answers usually sit in separate spreadsheets. This pack brings them together, with each page built around a specific management decision.

What's inside
PageAnswers
Model SummaryAre we on budget? How does revenue turn into EBITDA?
Sales AnalysisWhich categories and channels hit or missed budget?
Customer IntelligenceNew vs returning customers, discounts and refunds by customer
ProfitabilityWhich products earn the most after all costs?
Customer AgingWho owes us money, and how overdue is it?
What-if AnalysisWhat happens to EBITDA if assumptions change?

Why it matters

What this shows

  • The full FP&A cycle in one place: planning, reporting, variance analysis and forward-looking scenarios.
  • Scenario modelling built into the report itself, so leadership can test assumptions without a separate spreadsheet.
  • Working capital awareness: the aging page follows how credit control teams actually work.
  • Reports designed around the questions a CFO asks, not just charts of the data.

The company, customers, products and figures are fictional, created for this portfolio.
