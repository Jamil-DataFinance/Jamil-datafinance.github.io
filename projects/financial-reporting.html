Financial Reporting in Power BI

A complete set of financial statements (income statement, balance sheet and cash flow) built directly from the accounting database. The numbers refresh automatically, and the report checks itself to confirm the statements balance.

StatementsP&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow
Period2019–2021, yearly or monthly
ControlsBuilt-in accuracy checks
Built withPower BI, SQL, DAX
The problem

Month-end statements shouldn't be rebuilt by hand

Many finance teams export the trial balance to Excel every month, rebuild the statements, and check by eye that the balance sheet balances. It's slow, and every manual step is a chance for an error.

What I built

Live statements with their own controls

  • Connected Power BI straight to the SQL accounting database, so the reports update on refresh.
  • Built a properly laid-out income statement with subtotals (gross profit, EBIT, net income) and margin %.
  • Built the balance sheet and an indirect-method cash flow statement that reconciles to closing cash.
  • Added model checks confirming profit is correct, the balance sheet balances, and cash ties out.
  • The same report switches between yearly and monthly views.
