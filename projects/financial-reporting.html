01The problem

Month-end statements shouldn't be rebuilt by hand

Many finance teams export the trial balance to Excel every month, rebuild the statements, and check by eye that the balance sheet balances. It is slow, it depends on one person, and every manual step is a chance for an error that nobody notices until the auditors do.

02My approach

Live statements that check themselves

  1. Connect to the sourcePower BI reads the SQL accounting database directly, so statements update on refresh: no exports, no copy-paste.
  2. Lay the statements out properlyIncome statement with gross profit, EBIT and net income subtotals and margins; balance sheet; indirect cash flow reconciling to closing cash.
  3. Build in the controlsChecks confirm profit is right, the balance sheet balances and cash ties out, visible on the home page.
  4. One model, two viewsThe same statements switch between yearly reporting and monthly management accounts.
