01The problem

Staff costs need people data behind them

Payroll is usually the largest cost line, but what drives it, headcount by department and who is leaving and why, sits in HR spreadsheets finance rarely sees. Without it, headcount budgets and hiring plans are built on last year's numbers rather than on what is happening in the workforce.

02My approach

From HR records to planning inputs

  1. Clean the dataRemoved duplicates and filled gaps in Power Query.
  2. Make turnover comparableAge bands and an attrition rate, so groups of different sizes can be compared fairly.
  3. Four views for four questionsHeadcount overview, demographics, an individual review tracker, and attrition by year, role, age, overtime and gender.
