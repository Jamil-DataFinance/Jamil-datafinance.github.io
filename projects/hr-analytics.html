People analytics · Power BI

HR Analytics Dashboard

A four-page Power BI report on a 1,470-person workforce: who works where, how the workforce is made up, how each employee's reviews have moved over time, and where staff turnover is highest.

Workforce1,470 employees, 2012–2022
Pages4 report pages
CoversHeadcount, demographics, reviews, attrition
Built withPower BI, DAX, Power Query
The problem

Staff costs need people data behind them

Payroll is usually the largest cost line, but the information that drives it (headcount by department, who is leaving, and why) tends to sit in HR spreadsheets that finance never sees.

Without it, headcount budgets and hiring plans are built on last year's numbers rather than on what is actually happening in the workforce.

What I built
  • Cleaned the employee data in Power Query: removed duplicates and filled gaps.
  • Added age bands and an attrition rate so turnover can be compared fairly across groups.
  • A headcount overview by department and role, with hiring and leavers by year.
  • An employee tracker that shows one person's review scores over five years.
  • An attrition page showing turnover by year, job role, age band and gender.
