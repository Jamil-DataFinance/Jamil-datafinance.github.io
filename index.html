How I work

The question comes first

I'm a finance professional first, who knows how to use BI and data science tools to answer finance questions. Click a step.

Start from the decision, not the data

The hard part of finance work is knowing what management has to decide: are we on budget, why is cash behind revenue, which business unit needs a plan, can we afford the expansion. I write that question down before opening a single file.

Business partneringBudget vs actualIFRS reporting

Example: in the healthcare FP&A pack every page answers one stakeholder's question: CFO, finance team, COO or board.

Audit the data before trusting it

Four years in external audit taught me to reconcile before I report. Rows tie back to the source, totals tie to control figures, and gaps are investigated rather than hidden. Python and Power Query make those checks repeatable.

Python · pandasPower QuerySQLReconciliations

Example: the Python EDA reconciles 10,841 raw rows to 9,634, and the healthcare pack will not publish until 13 checks pass.

Explain the movement, not just the number

A number on its own says little. I break it down against budget, last year and constant currency, into price, volume and mix, and into the levers management controls, then test the scenarios.

Power BI · DAXExcel modellingScenario analysisStatistics

Example: the FP&A dashboard bridges revenue to EBITDA, and the 5-year model links all three statements to a DCF.

End with an action, an owner, and less manual work

Every report ends in a message: who should do what. Then I automate the routine so the next month takes less time. AI assistants help me build tools on dummy data, Python runs them on the real data, and Power Automate does the admin.

AI assistantsPython automationPower Automate

Result at work: 85% less time on monthly reporting and a month-end close 2 days faster. More in Automating Financial Reporting with AI.
